About Kapsule

Kapsule is a premium personal digital vault.

It helps people organize documents, receipts, warranties, subscriptions, notes, bookmarks and important life information.

Kapsule is not cloud storage.

Kapsule is not Google Drive.

Kapsule is not Dropbox.

Kapsule is not enterprise software.

Kapsule feels calm, premium, trustworthy and timeless.
