# Kapsule — Brand Personality

Purpose

This document defines who Kapsule is.

Not what it does.

Not how it looks.

Who it is.

Every design decision.

Every interaction.

Every animation.

Every sentence.

Every icon.

Every screen.

Should reflect this personality.
