# Kapsule Motion Constitution

Purpose

Motion communicates.

Motion never decorates.

---

Every animation must answer one question:

What changed?

---

Motion Principles

Fast.

Soft.

Natural.

Consistent.

Invisible.

---

Animation Duration

Micro

100–150ms

Standard

180–250ms

Large transitions

250–350ms

Never exceed 500ms.

---

Easing

Smooth.

Natural.

Never bouncy.

Never exaggerated.

Avoid playful motion.

---

Page Transition

Soft fade.

Subtle slide.

Maintain spatial continuity.

---

Cards

Lift slightly on hover.

Never jump.

Never bounce.

---

Buttons

Soft press.

Small scale.

Opacity change.

No flashy effects.

---

Modals

Fade + scale.

Never fly from random directions.

---

Sidebar

Slide naturally.

Respect user context.

---

Lists

Animate insertion.

Animate removal.

Preserve spatial memory.

---

Search

Results fade smoothly.

Never flicker.

---

Loading

Skeletons.

Progressive reveal.

Avoid spinners whenever possible.

---

Scrolling

Smooth.

Comfortable.

Predictable.

---

Gestures

Natural.

Immediate response.

No delay.

---

Golden Rule

If removing an animation improves the experience,

remove it.
