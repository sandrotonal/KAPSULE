# Kapsule — Reference Analysis

## Purpose

This document explains WHY each reference product exists.

These products are NOT templates.

They are NOT competitors.

They are NOT layouts to copy.

They are collections of world-class design decisions.

Extract principles.

Never copy interfaces.

Never recreate screens.

Never imitate branding.

---

# Design Philosophy

Kapsule should feel like a product that could naturally exist beside these applications.

Users should notice the same level of quality,

not the same interface.

Every inspiration must be invisible.

Every decision must become uniquely Kapsule.

##############################################################
Final Principle
##############################################################

Never ask

"Can we copy this?"

Always ask

"What principle made this product great?"

Then redesign that principle for Kapsule.

Kapsule must become its own product.

Not a combination of existing ones.

Every reference exists to improve judgment,

never to replace originality.
