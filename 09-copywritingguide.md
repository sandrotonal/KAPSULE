# Kapsule Copywriting Guide

Purpose

Defines every piece of text shown to users.

The product should sound calm.

Never robotic.

Never playful.

Never corporate.

---

Voice

Confident.

Helpful.

Minimal.

Warm.

Professional.

Human.

---

Tone

Quiet.

Respectful.

Simple.

Clear.

Trustworthy.

---

Avoid

Buzzwords.

Marketing language.

Technical jargon.

Corporate phrases.

Over-explaining.

---

Buttons

Prefer

Save

Open

Continue

Share

Archive

Delete

Restore

Rename

Import

Export

Done

Cancel

Avoid

Execute

Submit

Launch

Initiate

Synchronize

---

Empty States

Explain what this area is.

Explain why it matters.

Suggest the next action.

Never say only

"No data"

"No results"

---

Examples

No documents yet.

Import your first document to start building your digital archive.

---

Search

Instead of

"No matches"

Use

Nothing matched your search.

Try another keyword.

---

Errors

Explain.

Never blame.

Bad

Something went wrong.

Good

We couldn't upload your document.

Please try again.

---

Success

Short.

Quiet.

Examples

Saved.

Updated.

Receipt added.

Document archived.

Changes saved.

---

Notifications

Only when necessary.

Avoid notification fatigue.

---

Settings

Plain language.

Not technical language.

Bad

Authentication

Good

Security

---

Categories

Use human language.

Not database language.

---

Dates

Readable.

Yesterday

Today

Last week

June 14

Avoid timestamps everywhere.

---

Capitalization

Sentence case.

Avoid ALL CAPS.

Avoid excessive punctuation.

---

Microcopy Principles

One sentence is enough.

Every word should earn its place.

Clarity beats creativity.

Trust beats cleverness.

Silence beats noise.
